The point of view

The place comes first.
The photography follows.

Photography shapes the first impression of a property long before a guest arrives. Maison brings attention to architecture, natural light, the character of a room and the setting beyond it, creating imagery intended for websites, launches, campaigns and editorial use.

Ways to commission

Right-sized for
the place and the purpose.

Begin with the scale of the story you need to tell. These are starting points for a conversation, not preset packages or promises of fixed deliverables.

A defined assignment

Focused property shoot

For a property update, new interiors, amenities, architecture or a specific collection of images that need to be refreshed.

Typical creative scope

  • Prioritized spaces and photographic brief
  • Planned shoot and selective image editing
  • Final image use and licensing agreed in advance

Best suited to a clear, focused need.

A broader visual collection

Complete property story

For an opening, website relaunch or brand campaign requiring a connected visual narrative across an entire property or destination.

Typical creative scope

  • Creative planning across arrival, interiors and setting
  • Coordinated photography collection
  • Film and additional production scoped where appropriate

Best suited to a launch or larger story.

Scope, schedule, deliverables, licensing and fees are confirmed in a written proposal before any booking.

Working together

From a first brief
to the final image.

  1. Tell us about the property.

    Share the location, intended use, timing and the imagery you have in mind.

  2. Define the commission.

    We discuss priorities and production needs, then agree on a written scope and fee.

  3. Create the work.

    The photography or film is planned, produced, edited and delivered to the agreed brief.
