# Maison Krizsan V5: Editorial and conversion audit

## Guiding decision
The hero and photography establish aesthetic credibility. Copy identifies the actual offer within one line of the What We Do section, explains who commissions the work and how they can enquire.

## Changed
- Eliminated the cryptic “They haven't arrived. They're already deciding.” heading.
- Kept its underlying commercial insight in a plain, comprehensible sentence.
- Added a direct “Discuss a commission” CTA after What We Do.
- Rewrote the services introduction around a focused shoot vs. coordinated stills/motion collection.
- Changed process and contact copy to answer what the prospect should send and what follows.
- Removed hero location microcopy, keeping location once in the bottom utility footer.
- Built a high-impact desktop wordmark and stacked mobile wordmark, with complete and usable site navigation.
- Added accessible Privacy, Terms of Use and Accessibility information pages. No dummy legal links.

## Research distinctions
- California CalOPPA requires a conspicuous policy for applicable commercial sites collecting personally identifiable information. CalOPPA != CCPA business-threshold rules.
- No cookie consent banner was added absent advertising/analytics trackers in site code. Hosting, email and font providers may still have technical processing obligations.
- Terms of use are useful for explaining photography copyright and enquiry scope, not asserted as legally required.
- Privacy/terms are practical website texts, not a substitute for review by counsel, and must be checked against actual hosting, tracking and business operations before publication.
- No fictional LLC, registered company address, license, testimonials or awards inserted.
