# Maison commission lead operating system

A form submission is **not** revenue. Track it until an outcome exists.

1. **New:** A form email arrives in Zoho. Add one row in `LEAD_PIPELINE.csv` (or import that CSV as column headers into your existing spreadsheet). Do not copy sensitive client briefs into public repositories.
2. **Qualified / Hold:** Verify a real property, decision-maker, requested scope and plausible project timeline. Mark `Unknown` when an answer is unavailable; don't convert Unknown to unqualified.
3. **Discovery:** Reply to the prospect directly. Discuss location, goals, deliverables, licensing/use, timeline, access to property, travel and budget fit.
4. **Proposal:** Specify outputs, approvals, schedule, project fee, usage rights, revisions and payment terms in a separate agreement.
5. **Won / Lost / Hold:** Track actual signed scope and contracted value. A form success is not a paid project.
6. **Follow-up:** Every open opportunity gets a next action and due date. Do not silently lose open leads.

**Metrics by month:** visits to Maison (Cloudflare Web Analytics), delivered enquiry emails (Resend/Zoho), qualified enquiries, proposals sent, signed commissions and booked revenue. Derive meaningful conversion rates only when denominators are known. Resend delivery events are not necessarily distinct humans; deduplicate real leads in the sheet.

**Response service standard:** Aim for one business day when possible; do not publish an SLA you can't consistently meet.

**Security:** Keep the lead sheet private. Do not place prospects' names or email addresses into public analytics URLs, filenames, or logs.
