Accessibility

The work should be available to everyone who wishes to experience it.

Effective 16 October 2026

Our approach

Maison Krizsan aims to make this website usable with keyboards, clear navigation, readable text and alternatives for visual media. The homepage video has a still-image fallback and respects reduced-motion preferences. These features are design intentions, not a claim of formal accessibility certification.

Known limits

Photography is central to this portfolio, and written descriptions cannot fully communicate every visual detail. Browser, device and assistive-technology behavior may vary, and some barriers can remain.

Report a problem

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email studio@maisonkrizsan.com with the page and a brief description of the difficulty. We can discuss an accessible way to provide the information you need.
