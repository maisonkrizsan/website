# Maison V10 - Commission-ready presentation

## What this revision does
- Adds `/commission` as a shareable buyer-ready commission portfolio grounded in actual work.
- Separates **what Maison offers** (photography / film / direction) from **how a buyer can commission it** (a focused shoot or complete property story).
- Links the presentation from the homepage service context, early Work-page invitation, and site footers.
- Preserves the existing homepage form, `/enquire` alternative, Cloudflare Pages Function, security keys and full photography gallery.
- Does not invent prices, timelines, image counts, licensing grants, clients, awards, testimonials, or proof of filming projects.

## Commission formats
Formats are useful starting points, not priced packages or fixed-scope promises. Each actual project still requires a written scope specifying priority spaces, final deliverables, schedule, licensing and fees.

## Non-negotiable approval gates
- Photograph/publication rights for photographed locations and the portrait: **not established**.
- Film capability: described as available, but no actual hospitality video proof in provided source material.
- Real pricing, turnaround, team capacity: **not established**; do not fabricate.
- Hosting, Resend and Cloudflare live deployment: must be checked after upload.
- Conversion lift is an experiment hypothesis, not a reported result.
