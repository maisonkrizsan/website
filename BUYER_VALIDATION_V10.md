# Five-buyer conversion validation

**Objective:** Test whether Maison's buyer-ready presentation makes it easier for a hotel owner, hospitality marketing lead or property manager to envision commissioning the studio.

Send `https://maisonkrizsan.com/commission` to five **relevant** decision-makers (after deployment and permission checks). Ask only:
1. Which type of project would you consider Maison for?
2. What evidence or detail would you need before making an enquiry or recommending Maison internally?
3. Is anything unclear about the scope and next step?

Record actual responses, referral/forwarding interest, project enquiries, and whether proposals follow. Do **not** count a page visit or polite compliment as buying intent. Success is a qualified enquiry or concrete buying step. The content and positioning should only be revised in response to a pattern of real buyer objections.
