# Maison Krizsan · First verified hospitality proof project

## Objective
Create **one genuine commercial hospitality story** that demonstrates Maison's photography, filmmaking, planning and professionalism. A client relationship is not assumed. No award, conversion result, testimonial or permission may be implied before earned.

## Target
A visually distinctive independent hotel, boutique resort, architectural residence or hospitality operator with a decision-maker who values visual presentation and a realistic shoot schedule. Prefer a paid, defined-scope commission; if a strategic collaboration is explored, specify the full exchange in writing.

## Offer to pitch
**Maison Property Story:** a focused one-property photography and film commission designed around the property's site, launch or campaign. Example deliverables for negotiation:

- 10–15 edited architectural/hospitality photographs;
- a 20–45 second original-footage property film (not an animated still);
- one short property story for approved public use, describing the real project brief, the visual approach and the deliverables;
- optional social-media cutdowns if actually scoped.

Deliverables, pricing, usage rights, turnaround and approvals must be agreed in writing before the shoot. Do not guarantee booking results or business outcomes.

## Evidence to secure at contracting
- Written permission to publicly show approved imagery and name the property;
- clear credit and Maison's actual role;
- client permission to mention the project scope and deliverables;
- whether the client would consider providing feedback **after** delivery (no quote assumed);
- permission to use behind-the-scenes material if applicable.

## Minimum proof package after delivery
1. One extraordinary opening photograph.
2. A 30-second real-film sample with audio rights cleared.
3. Seven supporting frames including architecture, amenities, arrival and detail.
4. A concise factual summary: property/project, actual assignment, decisions, deliverables and usage.
5. Optional attributable feedback with explicit approval, never drafted on a client's behalf.

## Smallest next action
Select one qualified property and send a tailored, specific collaboration proposal showing 3 relevant Maison photographs and a clearly scoped commercial offer. A paid client response is external evidence; a beautiful new page alone is not.
